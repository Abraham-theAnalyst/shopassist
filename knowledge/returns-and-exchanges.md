# Returns and Exchanges

## Returning an item

You can return an item within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, with the tags attached.

## Refunds

Refunds go to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are free within Lagos.

## Sale items

Sale items cannot be refunded. They can only be exchanged for a different size.

## Items we cannot take back

For hygiene reasons, underwear and earrings cannot be returned.

## Return shipping costs

You pay the shipping cost for returns, unless the item is faulty.
