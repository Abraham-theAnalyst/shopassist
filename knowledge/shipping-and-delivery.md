# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery within Lagos

Delivery within Lagos takes 1 to 2 business days and costs ₦3,500.

## Delivery to other Nigerian states

Delivery to other states in Nigeria takes 3 to 5 business days and costs ₦6,000.

## Free delivery

Delivery within Nigeria is free on orders over ₦100,000.

## International delivery

We deliver to Ghana, the UK, and the US. International delivery takes 7 to 12 business days. The delivery cost is shown at checkout.

## Same-day dispatch

Orders placed before 12 noon, Monday to Friday, are dispatched the same day.

## Pickup in Lekki Phase 1

You can pick up your order at our Lekki Phase 1 pickup point for free. Pickup orders are ready within 24 hours.
